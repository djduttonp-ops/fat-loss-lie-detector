Macro Film Crew

Here's how food actually works.

You now have your calorie and protein numbers. But numbers mean nothing if you don't understand what you're spending them on.

Think of your food like a film crew. Calories are the production budget. Protein, carbohydrates and fat are the cast and crew. Every one has a job. Some are much easier to overbook than others.

Let's meet the crew.

One thing before we meet the cast: Carbs don't make you fat. Fat doesn't make you fat. Protein doesn't make you fat.

Too many calories, repeated often enough. That's what causes fat gain.

But here's why macros matter: each one makes it easy or hard to stay within your budget. Protein keeps you full and protects your muscle. Carbs are easy to overeat in large volumes. Fat is calorie-dense and easy to undercount. Alcohol pauses everything else while your body deals with it first.

The macro split isn't a moral position. It's a tool for managing the budget without going mad.
💰
Calories: The Production Budget
You can spend them on a decent cast, a strong lead, and scenes that actually move the plot forward. Or you can blow the whole budget on extras, snacks, drinks, sauces, and a weekend cameo that was never in the script. Go over budget often enough and the body stores the surplus. Stay under budget consistently enough and fat loss starts. No plot twist. Just maths with better lighting.
💪
Protein: The Lead Role
Turns up for every scene, holds the story together. Builds, repairs, keeps you full. When Protein walks off set, the whole film collapses and you wonder why you look deflated instead of lean.
⚡
Carbs: The Action Crew
Does the heavy lifting, keeps the action scenes alive. Cut them and everything slows. You're not clean eating. You're filming in slow motion and wondering why your brain forgot its lines.
🔧
Fat: The Producer
Works behind the scenes. Keeps hormones stable, moods level, face from cracking. Too little and everyone looks exhausted. Too much and the whole cast turns shiny.
🍻
Alcohol: The Uninvited Cameo
Shows up unannounced, drinks the props, forgets the lines. While your body burns it off, fat loss is on pause. The rest of the crew are in the trailer, bored.
🥤
Diet Soda. The Extra Who Thinks She's Main Cast
Diet Coke, Pepsi Max, Coke Zero. Zero calories, maximum attitude. Keeps showing up uninvited, whispering: "At least I'm not cake."
⚡
Energy Drinks. The Stunt Double Who Thinks It's The Star
Turns up loud, dramatic, and convinced it's carrying the scene. It can help you get through a tired afternoon, but it is not sleep, food, recovery, or a personality. If your whole performance depends on a neon can, the production schedule is already in trouble.

When It Goes Wrong: Because It Will

You have a plan. You are also a human being.

With a kitchen, a weekend, and feelings. Use this before you turn one wobble into a full personality collapse.

🍕I binged+
It happened. It's done. One meal doesn't erase a week, but deciding the day's ruined will. Continue at the next meal. No ceremony. No restart. The meal was a comma, not a full stop.
🍻The booze avalanche happened+
Cut carbs, drank, appetite spiked, cheeseboard, regret, restart Monday. Break the sequence right here. Today: hydrate, protein, move gently. Don't punish yourself into skipping meals. That starts the spiral again.
💪I missed training+
One missed session changes nothing. Two starts a pattern. Three is a new habit. Get back tomorrow. Don't double up to punish yourself. That's drama, not discipline. Just show up.
🏋️I'm training, but my body still isn't changing+
Go through this honestly. Not defensively.

Have the weights or reps actually increased in the last month?
Are the final reps genuinely difficult, or are you stopping when the song ends?
Are rest periods controlled, or is 90 seconds quietly becoming five minutes?
Is the phone there for music, or are you disappearing into it?
Is your technique improving, or just familiar?
Are you recording sessions anywhere, or relying on memory?
Are you training your whole body, or the same three favourites?
Are you eating back estimated exercise calories?

At your next session, pick one exercise and beat your last performance by one controlled rep, slightly more weight, better form, or a shorter rest. Give the body a reason to adapt. One thing. Not a new programme.
📱I followed someone online and it made things worse+
They're not a nutritionist. They're marketing with good lighting. Come back to the basics: protein, deficit, consistency. That's the whole thing. It was always the whole thing.
🚪I want to quit+
You're not quitting. You're tired of not seeing results fast enough. Every time you change the plan, you reset the clock. Don't quit. Stop interrupting.
📅I've already restarted this week+
Monday is not sacred. It is just a day. Progress dies when you restart the calendar instead of the habit. Continue. Not restart.
⚖️The scales haven't moved in two weeks+
Two weeks of no movement does not mean nothing is happening. Check your logging. Check your weekend. Check your sleep. Evidence first. Panic never.
